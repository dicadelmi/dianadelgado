Cunard Brochure 2026 - 29

Project Overview

For this brochure cover, I developed an Art Deco pattern rooted in the rhythm of ocean waves, the curve of seashells, a rising sun, and the silhouette of a ship. Repeating the pattern four times reinforced the Cunard's four-ship fleet, giving the design a deeper layer of meaning.
